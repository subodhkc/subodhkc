# Durable Brand Positioning Rule

All customer-facing work must follow `docs/BRAND_CANON.md`.

Preserve the commercial progression `Diagnose -> Advise -> Architect / Build -> Assure` and the identity `Enterprise AI Advisor + AI Systems Architect`.

Do not reintroduce low-price-first positioning, governance-first umbrella positioning, or legacy HAIEC expansions. Existing routes and commercial infrastructure may remain for compatibility, but public hierarchy must lead with consequential decisions and enterprise outcomes.

Before adding a new service name, prove that it cannot fit the canonical architecture. Before changing navigation, prove that the buyer, core problem, or commercial motion changed.
