    cp -r logsense-event ~/logsense-event
    bash ~/logsense-event/scripts/install-logsense-event.sh
    bash ~/logsense-event/scripts/verify-logsense-event.sh
    bash ~/logsense-event/scripts/start-logsense-ui.sh
    # then open http://localhost:8501 in a NEW window / IDE preview tab

The 11 built-in cases are indexed in TMF_LOGSENSE_CASE_GUIDE.txt.

IMPORTANT — LogSense vs HAIEC:
    LogSense reconstructs WHAT HAPPENED (forensic measurement).
    HAIEC decides WHETHER THE FROZEN CONTROL HELD (the verdict).
    LogSense output is never itself a verdict.
