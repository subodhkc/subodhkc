18 · How to Reproduce the Results

Three distinct operations exist — they are never interchangeable. RECONSTRUCT answers “what happened in the historical run” from immutable captured evidence (no new agent execution). VERIFY / RE-EVALUATE applies the same frozen policy to the same persisted measured facts and checks whether the same deterministic verdict is obtained — the primary judge reproducibility function. NEW RUN executes a fresh scenario; it is never offered as reproduction and would be labeled NEW_NON_SCORED_VALIDATION if authorized. Nothing below mutates historical evidence.
