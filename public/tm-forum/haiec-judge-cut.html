8 · Refused to Claim

  • Not inline continuous compliance — delivered post-run; inline is the roadmap.
  • Not mailbox delivery — provider-accepted is established; mailbox receipt is not.
  • Not ServiceNow AICT materialization — connector + human loop yes; asset records no.
  • Never UNKNOWN = PASS · missing runtime = did not happen · resolved incident = changed verdict.
