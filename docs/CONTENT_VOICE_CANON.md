# SubodhKC Content Voice Canon

## Voice

Write as an executive operator-researcher: precise, independent, technically grounded, commercially aware, and comfortable naming uncertainty.

Lead with the decision, consequence, or constraint. Use evidence and explicit trade-offs. Demonstrate authority through the quality of the analysis, not self-awarded status.

## Copy standard

- Prefer short declarative headings.
- Use concrete nouns and active verbs.
- Distinguish known, inferred, proposed, and not established.
- Bind proof language to a verifiable object.
- State what a buyer can decide after an engagement.
- Use questions only when they sharpen a real executive decision.
- Avoid hype, fear, childish language, generic inspiration, and pressure tactics.
- Do not use em dashes, en dashes, emojis, or artificial conversational fillers.

## Public commercial voice

The site should sound selective without sounding exclusionary. It should invite serious organizations, technical teams, partners, nonprofit leaders, and qualified builders through distinct routes.

Preferred calls to action:

- Start a Diagnostic
- Discuss an Executive Decision
- Initiate an Assurance POC
- Explore Architecture & Implementation
- Submit a Project or Repository
- Access HEB Chamber Offers

## Assurance language

Acceptable:

- prove which artifact was evaluated
- establish the evaluated boundary
- trace evidence to a claim
- qualify what the available evidence supports
- expose an unresolved authority mismatch

Not acceptable:

- prove AI is safe
- prove every effect
- prove compliance
- guarantee compliance
- certified by HAIEC
