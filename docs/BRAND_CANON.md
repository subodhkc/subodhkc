# SubodhKC Brand Canon

## Canonical identity

Subodh KC is an Enterprise AI Advisor and AI Systems Architect.

Primary positioning:

> Turn consequential AI decisions into defensible operating choices.

Signature:

> From possibility to decision. From decision to production.

Category narrative:

> Decisions. Systems. Evidence.

Subodh investigates before prescribing. He exposes hidden constraints, compares material options, pressure-tests assumptions, and follows qualified decisions into architecture and production.

## Commercial architecture

The durable public progression is:

> Diagnose -> Advise -> Architect / Build -> Assure

- **Diagnostic Intensives:** the preferred starting point when the real problem, opportunity, or constraint is not yet established.
- **Executive AI Advisory:** continuing judgment for interconnected decisions, investments, vendors, operating models, and roadmaps.
- **AI Systems Architecture & Implementation:** production architecture and delivery after a decision has earned implementation.
- **AI Assurance:** evidence-bound evaluation for consequential AI through HAIEC.

Public pages must not present a low-cost ladder as the primary commercial story. Existing productized and subscription offers remain operational and may be presented through qualified community or member routes.

## HAIEC relationship

HAIEC means **High Assurance In Every Consequence**.

Canonical HAIEC category:

> Evidence-Bound Assurance for Consequential AI

HAIEC establishes bounded claims from available evidence. It does not certify that AI is safe, prove universal compliance, or convert missing evidence into a favorable state.

Durable product language:

> AI System -> Evaluated Scope -> Evidence -> Capability -> Claim -> Assurance -> Decision Receipt

Signature research territory:

> Permission is not delegation.

Research claims must remain clearly separated from shipping product capability.

## Visual direction

Use restrained editorial typography, strong hierarchy, generous whitespace, and thin directional system lines. The Action-to-Consequence Atlas is the preferred visual motif:

> Request -> AI Agent -> Tool -> Authority -> API -> Consequence

Synthetic examples must be explicitly labeled. Never make a decorative or synthetic flow appear to be live runtime evidence.

## Language

Prefer: establish, bind, trace, reconcile, expose, constrain, evaluate, qualify, compare, attribute, preserve, narrow, resolve, pressure-test, inspect, consequential, decision-grade, bounded, traceable, attributable, operating boundary, evidence coverage, unresolved exposure, decision consequence, provenance, confidence boundary.

Avoid: generic consultant, fully compliant, safe AI, complete visibility, guaranteed, bulletproof, certified, world-class, visionary, guru, thought leader, revolutionize, unlock, game-changer.

## Stability rule

New engineering waves update evidence and capability descriptions. They do not trigger a new category, navigation model, or commercial architecture unless the buyer, core problem, or commercial motion changes.
